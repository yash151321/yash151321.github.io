Kevin Studio

A personal tool that creates short vertical videos and uploads them to my own TikTok account as drafts. I review every draft and decide whether to post it myself.

What it does

Kevin Studio is part of Kevin, a personal assistant that runs on my own computer. When I ask for a video, it writes a short script, adds a voiceover, edits in licensed stock footage or my own clips, and adds captions.

When I'm happy with a video, Kevin Studio can send it to my TikTok account as a draft. Nothing is ever posted automatically.

Personal & non-commercial

How it works

  1. I give Kevin a topic, for example “3 gym mistakes beginners make”.
  2. Kevin creates the video on my computer and shows me a preview.
  3. I ask for changes, or approve it.
  4. Kevin Studio uploads the approved video to my own TikTok account as a draft.
  5. I open TikTok, review the draft and post it myself, or delete it.

Your account only

It works with one TikTok account: mine. It isn't offered to the public and isn't a commercial service.

Minimal access

It only asks TikTok for basic profile information and permission to upload drafts.

You stay in control

Nothing is posted automatically. Access can be removed at any time in TikTok's settings.
