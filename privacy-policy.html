Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

1. About Kevin Studio

Kevin Studio is a personal, non-commercial tool. It is used only by its owner, with the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public.

2. Information it receives

When the TikTok account is connected, Kevin Studio receives only:

  • Basic profile information, such as the display name and avatar, to show which account is connected.
  • Permission to upload videos as drafts to that account.

It does not ask for or receive followers, messages, comments, analytics or any other account data.

3. How the information is used

This information is used only to connect the account and upload videos the owner has created and approved, as drafts. Nothing is posted automatically: the owner reviews every draft in TikTok and decides whether to post it.

4. Storage and security

The access tokens TikTok issues are stored securely on the owner's own computer, in the operating system's protected credential store. They are not kept on any server run by Kevin Studio.

5. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with anyone. Information is sent only to TikTok, which is needed to upload drafts.

6. Removing access

Access can be removed at any time in the TikTok app's settings, where connected apps are listed. Once access is removed, Kevin Studio can no longer upload to the account. The stored tokens can also be deleted from the owner's computer.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
